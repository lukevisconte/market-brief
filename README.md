# MARKET-BRIEF

An automated creation engine--running twice daily at prior to market open, and after market close--that pulls: index prices, watchlist quotes, and news, all from Finnhub, and has Claude write a short briefing at forwards directly to your email to keep you informed on micro-macro market trends for the day.

Market Brief Sections:
1. WHAT MATTERS TODAY
   This includes broad headlines, risk-on verses risk-off classifications, a brief index run down, leadership and breadth analysis, and a summary of critical macro, mega-cap, and bellwether movement.
2. MARKET DASHBOARD
   This section includes the following: US Equities (SPY, DIA, IMW, QQQ); Macro Proxies (GLD, USO, TLT, UUP); Global Proxies (EWJ, MCHI, EWG, EWU, EEM); and basic analysis of key relationships within those broader categories.
3. GLOBAL MARKETS
   A brief summary of international market catalysts, valuation discrepancies, and how the movement in international markets supports or contradicts domestic moves. This section also includes a read through of large-cap movers in international markets such as TSM, AVGO, ASML, etc.
4. MACRO FACTORS
   This section covers, in more depth, interest rates (TLT), Dollar Expectations (UUD), Commodities Tracking (GLD, USO, etc.) Section 4 also includes a summary of global risk in the international markets based on the changes in the aforementioned commodities.
5. LARGE-CAP MOVERS
   In section 5 a summary of specific bellwether stocks, along with other notable movers, is given. Analysis, catalysts, and possible variant perceptions are also given if applicable, along with news specific and cross-industry connections if that data serves to explain the reason behind fluctuations in equity prices.
6. SMALL / HIGH BETA MOVERS
   In this section a summary of specific smaller representative stocks are given, along with notably violent movers. Analysis is also given when applicable, but are usually linked to fluctuations in specific sub-sectors, or Index movements as a whole. Stock specific analysis for movement is given whenever appropriate.
7. MACRO--STOCK CONNECTIONS
   This section essentially serves to connect macro movement with specific stocks, sectors, or sub-sectors whenever applicable. This section tends to focus on USO influence in the energy sector, UUD influence in financial sectors and international / emerging markets, industrial sector sensitivity, and influences on the AI / semiconductor / microchips sectors typically via their supply chains with effects and analysis on the AI-capex trade and large P/E multiples.
8. INVESTOR TAKEAWAY
   This section is essentially a self-explanatory. It serves as a broad summary of what matters most to different investors, and the most critical / notable signals to be expected from / from the day.
9. WHAT MATTERS FOR THE OPEN / TOMORROW
   This section serves to provide a few specifics to watch closely, including geopolitical risk factors, interest rates, commodity pricing, and individual equities.

It is also important to note that an Anthropic LLM is used for the construction of this briefing, and is therefore required to mark (via **) any text that is analysis. 

It is also important to note this brief is comprised of research observations, not personalized financial advice.
